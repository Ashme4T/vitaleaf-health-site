Communication Strategy

Keeping everyone in the loop

Different channels for different audiences, all drawing from the same underlying data.

Talking to our own people

Every employee is an active part of the SDG 3 mission, not someone reading announcements after the fact.

Wellness dashboards
Real-time health and engagement metrics, shared with every team.
Quarterly town halls
Leadership reports progress against the roadmap and takes questions.
“VitaLeaf Pulse”
A monthly newsletter of impact stories pulled straight from field operations.
ESG-linked pay
Part of senior leadership compensation is tied to measurable SDG 3 outcomes.

Talking to everyone else

Transparency and numbers that check out, not campaigns. Investors, regulators, and the communities we serve see the same data.

Impact dashboard
Live SDG 3 metrics at vitaleafhealth.org/impact, updated continuously.
Annual Impact Report
GRI-aligned, referencing the UN Global Compact, for investors and regulators.
#HealthForAll
Ongoing storytelling from patients, clinic staff, and community partners.
Community events
Free screening days and local health fairs run with community groups.

VitaLeaf Health is a hypothetical company created for an academic group assignment on SDG 3, submitted to Dr. Lubna Rashid Malik. All figures, achievements, and plans on this site are illustrative; the SDG 3 statistics referenced are drawn from WHO, UN DESA, and Our World in Data.
