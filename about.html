What We Do

Four ways in, one goal

We work across preventive healthcare, digital health technology, and community development, with four service lines that address SDG 3 targets 3.1 through 3.8 at once.

  • Telehealth Platform

    A 24/7 virtual consultation and e‑prescription service connecting patients in underserved areas with licensed clinicians over low-bandwidth mobile technology.

  • Community Micro-Clinics

    Small-footprint, low-cost physical clinics providing maternal and child health screening, basic diagnostics, and referral services where hospital access is limited.

  • Mental Wellness App

    A free counselling tier, peer-support communities, and stigma-reduction content, built for individual consumers and corporate wellness partners alike.

  • Workplace Wellness Programs

    Corporate health packages sold to partner employers: preventive screening, mental health resources, and health-literacy training for employees.

Our vision

“A world where quality health and well-being is a right, not a privilege, for every person, in every community, regardless of income or geography.”

Our mission

“To make preventive care, mental health support, and essential health services accessible, affordable, and stigma-free through technology-enabled community health solutions.”
